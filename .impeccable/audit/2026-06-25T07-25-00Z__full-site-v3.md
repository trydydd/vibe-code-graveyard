## Detailed Findings by Severity

### P3 Polish

**[P3] Filter button touch targets slightly tight on mobile**
- **Location:** `style.css` line 468 (`.filter-btn` at 480px)
- **Category:** Responsive Design / Touch Targets
- **Impact:** At 480px, filter buttons are 0.7rem font with 0.3rem × 0.6rem padding. Effective touch area is ~30×28px, below Apple's 44px recommendation. Works fine — fingers aren't that precise — but could be more generous.
- **Recommendation:** Increase to `padding: 0.45rem 0.75rem` and `font-size: 0.75rem` for a ~44×36px touch area. Low priority.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable adapt style.css`
